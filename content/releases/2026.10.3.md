  The GraphRAG service capstone now answers structured and whole-graph questions too, and the

  - knowledge-graphs.graphrag-service
  - context-engineering.context-builder

**A GraphRAG service that answers every kind of question.** The Knowledge graphs capstone used
to leave out two things the track teaches. Your service now also turns a question into a
guarded query over the graph, with the names in it resolved like any other mention, and answers
questions about the graph as a whole from summaries of its communities. Both respect the date a
question is asked about, and every answer still cites its sources.

**Worked examples in the context builder.** The Context engineering capstone now picks worked
examples for each turn from a bank of past tickets, within their own token budget, and has to
place them without breaking the cached prefix it already keeps stable.

If you passed either capstone before, it stays passed. Your saved code may no longer pass the
new checks if you open it again.

**Pro opens everything.** On Pro, no quest waits on its prerequisites any more: start wherever
you like. The order on the map is still the recommended route.
