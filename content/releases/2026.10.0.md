Forgeway opens with 91 exercises. Every one is graded by real tests against code you write, and
every check says what it expected when it fails.

## The tracks

- **Foundations** — how language models behave, how to prompt them and how to call them from
  code: tokens, system prompts, structured output, tool use, retries and prompt injection.
- **Retrieval** — chunking, keyword and vector search, hybrid ranking, reranking and
  retrieval-augmented generation, through to agentic search.
- **Protocols & tools** — the Model Context Protocol at the wire level, from the JSON-RPC
  handshake to clients that bridge tools into a model safely.
- **Orchestration** — routers, chains, fan-out, evaluator loops, handoffs and agents that survive
  a crash.
- **Knowledge graphs** — typed triples, entity resolution, time, multi-hop questions and
  graph-plus-vector retrieval.
- **Memory** — summaries, episodic and semantic stores, write policies, isolation, forgetting and
  reflection.
- **Context engineering** — token budgets, delimiters, cache-friendly prefixes, just-in-time
  loading and cited provenance.
- **Evals & guardrails** — metrics, judges, confidence intervals and regression gates, and the
  guardrails that protect a system in production.

## Capstones

There are nine capstones. Each of the seven tracks past the foundations closes with one that
draws on everything before it, from a production RAG service to an MCP gateway and a support bot
that is evaluated and guarded end to end, and each guided project ends with one. Passing a whole
track, capstone included, earns its certificate.

## Guided projects

Two guided projects build one codebase over several milestones, each one opening on your own
passing work from the last:

- **Support copilot** — a searchable help centre, per-customer memory, budgeted and guarded turns,
  and an eval suite that decides each release.
- **Research agent team** — MCP servers for search and notes, a knowledge graph of what the team
  finds, a durable team of agents, and an evaluation and red-team suite that decides whether it
  may ship.

## Free and Pro

The foundations and the first exercises of every track are free, with every check, hint and
badge. The capstones, the guided projects and public certificates are part of Forgeway Pro.
